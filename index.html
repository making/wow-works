wow works

Seven browser works made from a single prompt, filed here exactly as each model generated them. Each is self-contained, with no build step.

  1. I Harmonia DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017 Planetary positions computed from Keplerian elements and sonified as music. Click a planet, drag to look, scroll to change the passage of time.
  2. II The Drowned Cathedral GLM-5.3-Flash-FP4-Local A WebGL2 cathedral beneath still water. Move the pointer to look around, click to let the sound in, scroll to drift.
  3. III 墨の宇宙 / Ink Cosmos GLM-5.3-Flash-FP4-Local An ink-wash universe that redraws itself at every touch. Click to release a burst of ink.
  4. IV Totality DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017 A total solar eclipse over still water, computed in real time. Drag to look, scroll through the shadow; the corona, the diamond ring and the twilight around the whole horizon.
  5. V Whale Fall DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017 A descent to a whale fall four thousand metres down. Your lamp is the only light; hold to sink faster, click to sound the water. Generated bone, light and audio, no assets.
  6. VI The Last Second DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017 A probe falls toward a black hole and never arrives. Its clock slows, its light redshifts, and the whole scene is heard through the falling clock. Drag to look, scroll to let it fall. Needs a static server.
  7. VII CHIRP DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017 Two neutron stars fall into each other, and the gravitational wave they emit is what you hear. Click to begin, headphones recommended; hold to slow time.

The prompt came from mia-ai.net/lab/experiments and was given verbatim. Source is kept at github.com/making/wow-works.

The prompt
Build me something when people see it, they will say "Wow". Must be immersive, when people see it, they will be stunned by what they see. This can be a video, or anything else you can think of. Be creative. Go wild. Dig deep. No AI slop. Something unique, think outside of the box. Think big. Think amazing. Something no other model done before. Be proud. Show your best. Don't disappoint.

Some works need WebGL2 or Web Audio; sound begins only after a gesture.
